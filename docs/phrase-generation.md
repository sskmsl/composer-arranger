# Phrase Generation

## Purpose

Phrase Generation proposes three independent 2–4 bar melodic ideas from the selected section's chords.
It is intentionally separate from Melody Generation:

- Melody Generation designs a complete section-level line and its long-range climax.
- Phrase Generation produces compact material with a clear entrance, development, and landing for later editing or combination in Logic Pro.

Phrase candidates are stored in `ComposerProject.phraseCandidates`; they never become an Active Melody or alter `sectionMelodyAssignments`.

## Reused infrastructure

- chord parsing and `HarmonicMap`
- deterministic `SeededRandom`
- song profile, section role, density, drama, key, and vocal range
- `MelodyNote` and planned tone roles
- melody similarity feature extraction
- Web Audio preview
- SMF Type 1 export and the read-only Piano Roll

Only stable boundary components are shared. The section-scale Melody generator is not run with a shorter duration.

## Phrase-specific planning

Each pool candidate receives a `PhraseIntent` before notes are generated:

- 2, 3, or 4 bar length
- contour
- rhythm character (`flowing`, `syncopated`, `breathing`, `sustained`)
- harmonic approach (`chord-anchored`, `common-tone`, `tension-release`, `anticipatory`)
- cadence (`resolved`, `open`, `suspended`, `carry-forward`)
- density, rest ratio, leap amount, climax position, and pickup
- a 3–5 note motif interval/rhythm seed

The available and weighted choices depend on Section Role, Song Profile, chord color, common tones, Density, and Drama. Categories are not assigned to final Pattern numbers.

## Note construction

1. Plan a motif and rhythm skeleton.
2. Develop the motif using contraction, inversion, and contour-directed drift.
3. Use strong beats as harmonic anchors.
4. Allow tensions, anticipation, common-tone holds, and weak-beat movement where the selected harmonic approach gives them meaning.
5. Recover in the opposite direction after a large leap.
6. Realize the planned cadence on the final note.

This keeps pitch, rhythm, space, direction, and landing causally related rather than randomized independently.

## Quality and diversity selection

Twelve candidates are generated by default. A phrase quality score evaluates:

- strong-beat harmonic fit
- leap recovery
- singable range
- duration variety
- rest and density fit
- cadence realization

Candidates below the quality floor (`55`) are excluded when at least three qualifying candidates exist.

The final three candidates are selected using a quality/diversity balance:

```text
quality × 0.68 + minimum diversity × 0.32
```

Similarity reuses the full-melody comparison of opening, interval sequence, rhythm, contour, phrase shape, climax, cadence, and harmonic response. Phrase Intent overlap is added so candidates with superficially different notes but the same compositional interpretation are penalized.

Individual regeneration creates a new eight-candidate pool and selects against the two sibling phrases. It does not edit another phrase in place or derive it from Phrase 1.

## UI and output

The `Phrase` tab uses the selected section and existing generation settings. The only optional input is phrase length (`Auto`, 2, 3, or 4 bars).

Each candidate supports:

- individual audition, with or without chords
- individual regeneration
- a read-only Piano Roll
- individual MIDI export on a `Phrase` track

Detailed editing remains a Logic Pro responsibility.

## Known limits

- generation starts at the beginning of the selected section; arbitrary source-range selection is not yet provided
- phrase candidates are not automatically assembled into a section melody
- “human quality” is approximated through musical constraints and still requires neutral audition
- lyric prosody and imported MIDI are outside this phase
